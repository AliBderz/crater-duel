Crater Duel

Two guns on one hill. Set your elevation and charge, mind the wind, and fire. Every shell digs a crater, and the ground remembers.

Opponent

← → elevation · ↑ ↓ charge · Space fire · or drag on the field to aim

Gun manual

Elevation
0° fires flat to the right, 90° straight up, 180° flat to the left.
Charge
Muzzle speed. At 100% and 45° a shell crosses the whole map in still air.
Wind
Changes every turn and pushes the shell sideways the whole way. The gauge points the way it blows.
Damage
Up to 55 for a direct hit, less with distance. Falling into a fresh crater hurts too.
The CPU
Before each shot it simulates about 1,300 trajectories in the current wind, picks the closest, then adds a wobble so it can miss.
